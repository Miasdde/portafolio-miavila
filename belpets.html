Integrated Creative Campaign · 2026

Because They’re Family

A creative campaign designed to communicate trust, care and closeness between BelPets and the families who rely on the brand to care for their pets.

01 · Project Overview

Creating a warmer and more human connection between the brand and pet owners.

BelPets is a pet store and grooming business based in Santa Lucía, Honduras. The campaign was designed to strengthen its communication by presenting pets not simply as animals, but as meaningful members of the family.

The project included campaign concept development, graphic content, audiovisual ideas, social media materials, mockup development, an updated brand manual and KPI planning.

Client

Location

02 · The Challenge

Moving beyond product communication.

The campaign needed to communicate more than the services and products offered by the store. It required an emotional message capable of building trust and presenting BelPets as a reliable place for the care, grooming and wellbeing of pets.

03 · The Concept

Because They’re Family.

The campaign concept recognizes the emotional role pets have in people’s lives. Every visual and audiovisual piece was developed around care, closeness, responsibility and the idea that pets deserve the same attention as any other member of the family.

04 · Visual System

A friendly, recognizable and consistent visual language.

The existing BelPets identity was organized and expanded through an updated brand manual, clearer applications, consistent typography and a controlled color system.

05 · Social Campaign

Useful content designed with a close and approachable tone.

The graphic pieces combined practical pet-care information with direct messages, simple visual hierarchies and a consistent use of the brand’s identity.

Visualizing how the identity lives across physical touchpoints.

08 · Final Outcome

A cohesive campaign built around care, trust and emotional connection.

“Because They’re Family” brought together strategic communication, graphic content, audiovisual storytelling and practical brand applications. The result was a warmer and more consistent system for communicating the BelPets experience.
