MÍA 2026

I’m Mía Ávila, a visual storyteller from Honduras who believes the strongest ideas begin with empathy. Whether I’m building a brand, directing a campaign or photographing everyday moments, my goal is to create work people can connect with and remember.

I combine strategy, design, photography and motion to shape visual stories that are both engaging and meaningful. Outside the studio, I enjoy traveling, documenting places and finding inspiration in small details.

I’m currently looking for opportunities to learn, collaborate with creative teams and contribute to projects with purpose.
